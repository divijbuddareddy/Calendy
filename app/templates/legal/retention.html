We keep personal data only as long as the purposes in our privacy notice need it. Automated jobs enforce the periods below. Workspace admins can shorten or extend booking retention in Admin → Security.

Legal holds

We may keep data longer where the law requires it (for example tax records for payments) or to establish or defend legal claims.

Requests

Hosts can export or delete their data from Settings → Data & privacy. Workspace admins can delete data for specific invitees in Admin → Data deletion. Anyone can email {{ contact }}.
