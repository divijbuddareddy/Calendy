Privacy notice

Version {{ versions.privacy }} · Operated by {{ company }}

{{ app_name }} is a scheduling service. This notice explains what personal data we process for hosts (people with an account who publish booking links) and invitees (people who book a meeting through a link, without an account), why we process it, how long we keep it and the choices you have.

Roles

For account and billing data we are the controller. For booking data that invitees submit to a host, the host's organisation is the controller and we act as its processor. Invitees should also read the host's own privacy terms.

Sub-processors

Depending on what the host enables: cloud hosting and managed database (for example Render), calendar providers (Google, Microsoft), video providers (Google Meet, Zoom, Microsoft Teams), payment providers (Stripe, PayPal), email and SMS delivery providers, and error monitoring. Data is shared only as needed for the feature the host turned on.

Cookies

We use one strictly necessary, HTTP-only session cookie for signed-in hosts, plus a CSRF token. A lang cookie may remember the language you picked on a booking page. We do not use advertising cookies.

Retention

See our data retention policy (version {{ versions.retention }}) for how long each category is kept.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your data. Hosts can export their data and delete their account from Settings → Data & privacy. Invitees can cancel bookings with the link in their confirmation email, and can ask the host, or us at {{ contact }}, to access or erase their data. You can also complain to your local data-protection authority.

Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt. OAuth tokens and webhook secrets are encrypted at rest. Every database query is scoped to your workspace, traffic is encrypted in transit, and databases are backed up daily with point-in-time recovery.

International transfers

Where data leaves the UK or EEA we rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses with our providers.

Changes

When this notice changes materially we publish a new version here. We record which version each invitee accepted.
